# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

As the Eagle Technology founding team and collaborators, we are committed to an honest, respectful, and constructive engineering and creative environment—consistent with our core values of **Integrity**, **Collaboration**, **Accountability**, and **Excellence**.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate progress, blockers, and technical concerns early and respectfully.
- Give and receive code and design feedback constructively, focusing on quality and user outcomes.
- Respect each team member's primary discipline while supporting shared company goals.
- Protect company intellectual property, customer data, and confidential inquiries.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks in issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Publishing unverified claims, fabricated metrics, or unauthorized third-party content.
- Bypassing code review or security checks on production branches.

## Reporting

Any concerns may be raised directly with the Founder & CEO or founding team leadership for prompt and fair resolution.
