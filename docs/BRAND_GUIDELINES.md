# Eagle Technology Brand Guidelines

**Official Slogan:** *See Further. Build Smarter.*

---

## 1. Logo System

Clean vector and raster assets live in `public/brand/` and `src/components/ui/EagleLogo.tsx`:

- `public/brand/eagle-logo.svg` — Full emblem + geometric `EAGLE` wordmark
- `public/brand/eagle-mark.svg` — Emblem mark without wordmark (compact headers, favicons)
- `public/brand/eagle-logo-dark.png` — Transparent Dark Brown (`#4B2E0F`) raster export
- `public/brand/eagle-logo-white.png` — Transparent White (`#FFFFFF`) raster export for dark backgrounds

### Usage Rules

- Keep clear space around the emblem equal to at least the diameter of the outer circuit node.
- Do not stretch, rotate, add drop shadows, or recolor outside the official brand palette.
- On light backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`, `#FBF8F3`), render the mark in Dark Brown (`#4B2E0F`) with optional Brown Gold (`#B87333`) circuit node accents.
- On dark backgrounds (`#241507`, `#4B2E0F`), render the mark in White (`#FFFFFF`) with Brown Gold (`#B87333`) accents.

## 2. Official Color Palette

- **Dark Brown:** `#4B2E0F` (`--color-dark-brown`)
- **Brown Gold:** `#B87333` (`--color-brown-gold`)
- **White:** `#FFFFFF` (`--color-white`)

### Supporting Palette

- **Deep Brown:** `#241507` (`--color-deep-brown`)
- **Warm Off-White:** `#FBF8F3` (`--color-warm-off-white`)
- **Soft Sand:** `#EADCCB` (`--color-soft-sand`)
- **Muted Brown:** `#765B40` (`--color-muted-brown`)
- **Pale Gold:** `#F4E7D6` (`--color-pale-gold`)

---

## 3. Tone of Voice

- **Clear & Human:** Avoid buzzwords and unnecessary jargon.
- **Confident & Approachable:** Communicate competence without arrogance.
- **Honest:** Never overstate team size, completed client volume, or unverified metrics.
